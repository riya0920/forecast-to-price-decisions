gbm              +0.0057  +0.5051

Why this table sits next to accuracy: replenishment is asymmetric. A +5%
bias is 5% more inventory dollars on every item, every cycle, forever; a
-5% bias is a stockout rate that no service-level formula will predict
because the formula assumes an unbiased forecast. Variance you buffer,
bias you cannot -- safety stock sized on sigma does not cover a drift.

(1 series had no sales in the selection window and fall back to seasonal_naive.)

THE RESULT THIS SECTION EXISTS FOR: the plain WMAPE gate is WORSE than
just deploying the GBM everywhere. It picks 'naive' for most intermittent
series, and on a series that is mostly zeros 'last value' is usually zero,
so the forecast is all-zeros -- which scores WMAPE ~1.0 and cannot be beaten
downward by anything that ever guesses a nonzero day wrong. WMAPE rewards
the degenerate forecast on intermittent demand. Its selection-window bias
gives it away, and that is what the guard reads.
